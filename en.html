AI Toy · Child Companion · Parent-Guided Learning

An AI companion toy that talks, plays, and learns with children.

Dream W is building an AI-powered toy for young children. Instead of simply playing recorded audio, the toy uses an embedded AI chip to hold natural conversations and guide learning based on the topics parents choose.

Product

Learning through conversation, play, and curiosity.

Our goal is to make AI feel like a warm companion rather than another screen. Children can chat with the toy, ask questions, hear stories, and practise learning topics selected by their parents.

Natural Conversation

The toy responds to children’s questions, emotions, and curiosity in a friendly voice that encourages expression and exploration.

Parent-Guided Topics

Parents can choose learning preferences from a simple menu, such as English, maths, vocabulary, numbers, stories, or everyday knowledge.

AI Chip Interaction

Built-in AI allows the toy to go beyond fixed recordings, continuing the conversation, asking follow-up questions, and adapting explanations.

For Parents

Parents set the direction. Children enjoy the interaction.

Dream W is designed around a simple idea: parents choose the learning goals, while the toy turns those goals into everyday conversations and playful moments.

01

Choose a topic, such as early maths, English vocabulary, speaking practice, or stories.

02

Set preferences based on the child’s age, interests, and current learning goals.

03

Let the child interact through chat, questions, mini games, and encouraging feedback.

Why It Matters

We are not just teaching facts. We are helping children speak, ask, and explore.

Less Screen Dependence

A physical toy creates voice-first interaction, giving children a way to learn without constantly looking at a screen.

Warmer Early Learning

Learning can begin with a child’s simple “why”, followed by explanations that match their level and curiosity.

Family Involvement

Parents can bring learning goals into daily play, making education part of the home environment instead of only the classroom.
